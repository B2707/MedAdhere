# MedlinePlus official-terms snapshot and reviewed boundary

Captured from the U.S. National Library of Medicine's official pages on 2026-08-11:

- https://medlineplus.gov/about/using/usingcontent/
- https://medlineplus.gov/xml.html

Exact official excerpts:

> Summaries on health topic pages

> Source: MedlinePlus, National Library of Medicine.

The official content guidance identifies English and Spanish health-topic summaries as U.S.+federal public-domain content, requires source acknowledgment, and distinguishes copyrighted
MedlinePlus content. The official XML page welcomes download and reuse, requires MedlinePlus.gov
attribution, and identifies the health-topic XML fields, including metadata, vocabulary, full
summary, related topics, bilingual mappings, and site records.

Reviewed MedAdhere boundary:

- Only NLM-authored health-topic metadata, vocabulary, relationships, and full summaries are
  included.
- A.D.A.M. encyclopedia material, ASHP drug monographs, protected media and logos, and linked
  third-party site-record bodies are excluded.
- Every rendered summary identifies MedlinePlus.gov and the National Library of Medicine and links
  to the source topic.
- The corpus supports bounded condition context only. It is not a diagnosis or emergency-triage
  authority.
- The exact August 8, 2026 XML is byte-locked in `MANIFEST.json`.
