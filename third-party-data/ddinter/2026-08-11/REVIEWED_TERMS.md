# DDInter 2.0 official-terms snapshot and reviewed boundary

Captured from the publisher's official terms page on 2026-08-11:
https://ddinter2.scbdd.com/terms/

Official page section: `Data licensing`

Exact official excerpt:

> The DDInter data is made available under a Creative Commons
> Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 International license.

The same official page limits downloading to personal, non-commercial, informational, or scholarly
use. Its disclaimer says the database can be incomplete or erroneous and that absence of an
interaction does not establish that none exists.

Reviewed MedAdhere boundary:

- Licence identifier: `CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0`.
- Use is limited to this noncommercial academic project.
- Raw data and derived mappings stay outside the application's MIT code boundary.
- Use is deterministic clinician-facing drug-drug lookup only.
- No DDInter row or derived mapping is sent to a model or rendered as patient-facing advice.
- No-record means only no record within this exact frozen release, never safety.
- The eight official download-page files are listed and byte-locked in `MANIFEST.json`.
