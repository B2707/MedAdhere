# Health Canada DPD reviewed licence boundary

Reviewed from `https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada`, the official Drug Product Database API guide, and the official product-monograph FAQ on 2026-08-11.

The Open Government Licence - Canada permits copying, modifying, publishing, translating, adapting, distributing, and otherwise using licensed information for lawful purposes, including commercial use, subject to attribution and the stated exclusions. It does not grant rights in personal information, third-party rights the provider cannot license, official marks, patents, or trademarks.

This release contains only the six exact DPD product metadata records listed in `dpd-product-metadata.json`: DIN, status and dates, brand, ingredient names, dosage forms, routes, ATC codes, and product-monograph link/date metadata. The linked product-monograph PDFs are sponsor-authored documents. Their bodies are not copied, transformed, redistributed, indexed, or sent to the model.

Health Canada states that it reviews product monographs but the drug sponsor remains responsible for their completeness and accuracy. MedAdhere therefore stores these links as clinician-only candidate metadata, not as patient-facing evidence or medical advice.
