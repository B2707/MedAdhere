# CCDD reviewed licence boundary

Reviewed from `https://open.canada.ca/en/open-government-licence-canada` and the official CCDD dataset record on 2026-08-11.

The Open Government Licence - Canada permits copying, modifying, publishing, translating, adapting, distributing, and otherwise using the licensed information for any lawful purpose, including commercial use, subject to attribution and the stated exclusions. The licence does not grant rights in personal information, third-party rights the provider cannot license, official marks, or patents/trademarks.

This release contains the five official Health Canada CCDD CSV resources listed in `MANIFEST.json`: MP, NTP, TM, and the English/French MP-NTP-TM relationships. Device NTP, special groupings, and coded attributes are excluded. It also contains a six-row MedAdhere-reviewed factual identity map connecting selected CCDD/DIN products and therapeutic moieties to public RxNorm ingredient identifiers. That derived map is identified separately in the manifest and is not represented as Health Canada-authored data. Runtime lookup uses the frozen release locally; it never calls the source service.
